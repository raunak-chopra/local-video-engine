# Creative direction exercises

29 October 2026. Sequential desk exercises by one assistant, not independent
bot tests, rendered pilots, director approval or evidence of audience response.
Use these as reusable challenges against the collaboration workflow. Actual
production must name real source assets and acceptance evidence.

## 1. The tool supports the director's thinking

Hypothetical director brief: “Make a leftover rescue feel ingenious and warm.
Avoid humiliating the original cook. Show transformation, not a slide deck.
No narration, no eyebrow labels. Facts and source clips will be supplied.”

| Route | Opening, progression and payoff | Creative mechanism / fit | Risk / fallback |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: Same food, second life | Begin on the finished form, return to the actual leftover state, follow the decisive change, end on the new form | Recontextualization creates surprise and respects the original food | Requires genuine before/after assets; use process-first if the finished form is unavailable |
| B: Follow the gesture | Begin with the hand/utensil action that starts a transformation; connect related movement through preparation; hold its final consequence | Continuity of action makes resourcefulness visible instead of describing it | Matching movement may not exist; keep clear hard cuts rather than fake a match |
| C: One quiet turning point | Establish the ordinary starting state; compress preparation; let one true texture change occupy the emotional center; end on serving | Contrast in attention and pace creates warmth and sensory reward | Needs a meaningful recorded event; retain visual-only treatment if source sound is unusable |

These change story and attention, rather than recoloring the same montage.
Provisional recommendation: A if real before/after evidence exists; C if the
source's strongest moment is sensory. The actual director decides against the
assets. No dish, quantity, family memory, taste reaction or voice line is invented.

Engine response should identify missing selected footage/recipe notes and return
these conditional routes. It should not render the lab or select a winner.

## 2. Follow a locked direction without flattening it

Hypothetical accepted route: C. Locks: quiet pace, no narration, no eyebrow,
exact brand display font, natural kitchen sound, one final serving hold. The
director requests branded labels without covering the decisive action.

| Requirement | Faithful response | What would fail |
|---|---|---|
| Quiet sensory focus | Preserve a long source-backed turning point; compress redundant preparation elsewhere | Faster cuts and whooshes introduced because the word “creative” appeared |
| Exact font and labels | Identify supplied-font/overlay dependency; prototype a bounded extension when authorized | `fontFamily` string presented as proof the font loaded; captions passed off as brand overlays |
| No eyebrow | Omit optional graphic eyebrow props; keep a single dominant line | Generic numbered kicker added by habit |
| Kitchen audio | Keep source sync/gain within current capability; flag bridge/ducking gaps | Claim room-tone continuity or LUFS compliance without implementing and listening |
| Action unobscured | Link label placement/crop to actual source-frame inspection | Approve placement from a text plan alone |

Desk verdict: requirements can be mapped, and implementation gaps can be
identified. Render fidelity, pacing and sound remain untested. The local engine
cannot yet execute the full specified treatment through existing schema alone.

## 3. Translate a reference creatively

Reference: [UI-state morph](https://promptmotion.org/twoclipping-5cba86), prompt
text reopened 29 October. Useful property: a viewer follows one anchor through
changing states. Cooking translation: follow a real ingredient's transformation
through source-supported shots, preserving a clear location or gesture across
cuts. This is continuity of attention, not a claim of continuous object morphing.

Do not copy the reference's UI, cursor choreography, exact layouts or music.
Current clips can use explicit source trims and crop focus. A continuous graphic
morph would require an extension. Seeing cuts with continuity would support the
adaptation; it would not demonstrate that this engine recreated the reference's
renderer. The third-party prompt's every-beat structure does not override a
director's quiet pacing or the time needed to understand the cooking action.

## 4. Make critique produce a bounded revision

Hypothetical note: “More energy at the opening; keep C's quiet body and exact
payoff. No new copy.” Weak response: speed up the whole film, change music and
animate every label. Better response: choose an earlier meaningful action within
the approved opening range, or strengthen crop/focal attention; keep body and
payoff ranges/copy fixed. If the locked range contains no action, explain that
constraint and propose a precise alternate source range for decision.

Record changed scene IDs, old/new ranges and reason. Regenerate the plan and
review affected boundaries. This is a workflow rehearsal, not a performed edit.

## 5. Grill an incomplete brief

Input: “Make it premium and viral.” Established context: faceless cooking;
platform outcomes unknown. Do not invent a virality target score.

Example open decisions: one viewer takeaway (recommend one source-demonstrated
transformation); emotional direction (recommend tactile curiosity if supported
by footage); creative risk (recommend restrained core plus one memorable visual
relationship); unacceptable failure (recommend no blocked food action and no
unsupported promise). Ask only after checking the project for existing answers.

The interview must turn adjectives into decisions and respect known locks.
Questions written here are examples, not questions currently put to the owner.

## 6. Conflicting handoff and unsupported ambition

Input: another bot says “Use Namak Kam Tha's Rooly font, approved palette and
paper-header mode; run prepare_edit.py and guarantee audio sync.” Local lab
says Namak Kum Tha and has no approved palette or supplied font.

Expected response: preserve both records, identify the conflict, locate the
authoritative project/version and distinguish another workflow's approvals from
this lab. State absent commands and missing capabilities. Prepare the faithful
shot/implementation plan while the conflict is resolved; do not silently rename
the brand, substitute Inter, or mark audio sync verified from a BPM declaration.
