# Reel playbook (cooking, faceless, music + text)

Status: DRAFT, updated with primary-source research on 2026-10-13. Read
[the evidence and practical creative guide](REEL-CREATIVE-RESEARCH-2026-10-13.md)
for source dates, limits, hook examples, story beats and testing conventions.
Creative timings below are house hypotheses, not official performance thresholds.
Use comparable account Insights and playback review to evaluate them.

Execution scope and dependencies: [final update plan](REELS-FINAL-UPDATE-PLAN.md).
Stage 1 must support 30–60 s recipes and 3–10 s own-footage meme-style reels,
including reaction images/clips and optional stings from `assets/memes/` / `assets/sfx/`.
Hook-first, truthful tension, loop-match and send-line controls are supported
options; their presence in the engine does not require using them in every reel.

## 1. What Instagram says it ranks on

Primary source verified now: [Meta's Reels Chaining system card](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-reels-chaining/),
updated 2025-11-11, includes predictions for early skipping, watching relative
to same-length reels, resharing and following. Models change and are personalized;
the card does not provide a fixed creator-facing formula or signal weights.

Track **average watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach and skip rate**
when exposed. Keep duration and exact metric definitions. Do not substitute
aggregate shares for sends or infer superiority from watch seconds alone.

Historical secondary-source summary: the January 2025 "watch time, likes,
sends" shorthand and connected/unconnected audience distinctions were not
independently checked against the original post here. They are background,
not the operational source of ranking weights.

Not confirmed officially: saves as a top signal, exact weights ("sends are 3-5x
likes"), and any January 2026 "new hierarchy". Use as directional only.

## 2. Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

- Open with a relevant, striking action or result. A sizzle or sound hit is
  optional; a slow setup needs a reason. Make the subject apparent immediately.
- Use early text when it clarifies the promise; visual-only hooks are valid.
  Check comprehension muted and keep text readable.
- Hook patterns (templates, not proven):
  - Constraint: "Bache hue chawal ka naya dinner" when supported by the recipe.
  - Useful correction: "Crispy chahiye? Yeh step mat skip karo" with a demonstrated technique.
  - Contrast: "Same masala, alag result"
  - Specific curiosity: "Is crunch ka secret?" with a clear answer in the reel.
  - Relatable meme setup for the 3-10 s format
- Pair the hook with a source-backed payoff. Use early abandonment when available
  alongside audience fit and full playback review; likes alone do not diagnose it.

## 3. Retention and story tension

- Structure: **hook -> meaningful progress -> payoff**. The question can be
  how to achieve a dish shown early; an end reveal is optional. Deliver partial
  answers during the process and resolve the opening promise.
- Use truthful transformation, contrast or a real mistake/fix for tension.
  Timers need real recipe support; generic "wait for it" text is optional.
- Cut by action and comprehension. Compress repetition, hold useful technique
  and sensory payoffs longer, and align to music when it preserves meaning.
- Retention drops suggest hypotheses, not causes. Inspect the timestamp,
  readability, sound and audience mix before attributing a drop to pacing.
- Fifteen watch seconds on a 30 s reel versus eight on a 10 s reel is not
  enough evidence to predict which receives better distribution.
- Choose duration from the viewer task and footage. Short moments and longer
  recipes are formats to test, not universal reach tiers.

## 4. Loops and rewatches

- When a loop serves the idea, make the ending return smoothly to the opening
  while preserving a complete payoff. A loop is optional.
- Repetitive cooking actions (stirring, chopping, pouring) loop naturally.
- A rewatch may reflect pleasure or confusion. Keep the payoff complete;
  test a loop without assuming it improves reach.

## 5. Sound and ASMR

- Close-up cooking sounds (sizzle, chop, pour, crunch) layered under music.
- Align text/cuts to useful beats when the action remains understandable.
- Layer music, selected cooking ASMR, edit SFX and reaction stings with explicit
  gain/fades; lower music for key sounds and avoid doubling recorded sound.
- Always design for sound-off too: text must carry the meaning.
- Trending audio is a separate lever; pick it from Instagram's own audio tab at
  posting time. Licensed Envato music may not be eligible for that; the two
  strategies can run side by side.

## 6. On-screen text (Hindi / Hinglish / English)

- Large, high-contrast, inside the safe area (64 px minimum margin; keep clear
  of the bottom caption area and right-side buttons).
- At 1080×1920, start with house text insets top 192, right 160, bottom 420,
  left 64 px; configure them and verify the target app. These are not official
  universal UI dimensions.
- Use one idea per screen. Rough word/line limits are aids, not readability
  checks; inspect actual wrapping and reading time at phone scale.
- Mixed Devanagari and Hinglish/English lines use explicit font choices per
  text run, preserving conjuncts and marks; wait for all chosen fonts to load.
- Choose script from audience preference and readability; do not infer
  language preference from the dish's cultural style. Test each.
- Ingredient lists and step numbers on screen make the reel saveable.

## 7. Sends and saves (the growth levers)

- Design for **sends**: a relatable situation, practical solution for a specific
  person or enjoyable transformation. An optional send prompt supports that value.
- Design for **saves**: supplied recipe details in readable overlays or the post
  caption. An ingredient card needs enough reading time.
- Deliver the payoff before an optional, light call to action. Choose one
  primary action; do not force a CTA into every final second.

## 8. Originality: important for the meme plan

[Meta's May 2024 Instagram announcement](https://about.fb.com/ltam/news/2024/05/ayudando-a-los-creadores-a-encontrar-nuevas-audiencias/)
describes replacing identical reposts with originals and limiting repeat
aggregators. Substantial transformation is distinguished from identical reposts.
Check current Account Status and guidelines; this research did not verify the
previously cited April 2026 extension. Insert duration or attribution alone
does not guarantee recommendation eligibility.

Decision (owner, 2026-10-13): all reels are original content (own footage,
text, music and edit). Meme images and short reaction clips are used only as
emotion accents inside those reels, never as the main content.

Guidelines:
- Keep a reaction insert short (0.5-2 s) and let your own footage carry the
  rest of the reel.
- Place it at an emotional beat: the reveal, the mistake, the first bite.
- Start with at most 1-3 inserts as a house preference; this is not a platform
  threshold. Remove any insert that distracts from the cooking story.
- Your own text, joke and edit should be on screen over or around the insert.

## 9. Posting and testing routine

- Keep a log of hook, length, music, skip rate, average watch time and sends
  per reach when available. Compare similar formats/durations and audience
  mixes; recipes and short meme moments are separate groups.
- Change one major variable at a time (hook, length, music, text language).
  Sequential organic tests are not randomized A/B experiments.
- Review at consistent observation ages, such as 24 h, 72 h and seven days.
  Keep absent metrics unknown and record paid/organic status.
- Instagram trial reels (shown to non-followers first) are a safe place to
  test hooks; verify availability on your account.

## 10. What this means for the engine (build backlog)

| Feature | Why |
|---|---|
| Opening variants: relevant food/action shot, optional text and sound | Test early attention |
| Beat candidates with action-based overrides | Test pacing without losing clarity |
| Story beats and source-backed turning point; optional truthful timer | Meaningful tension |
| Optional loop-match ending with complete payoff | Test rewatch value |
| Ingredient card and step badges scene | Saves |
| Optional action line after payoff (Devanagari/Hinglish/English) | Support viewer intent |
| `reactionInsert` overlay: meme image or short clip + sting at a chosen frame, in any scene | Emotion accents inside original reels |
| `meme` short format (3-10 s) built on your own footage with a reaction insert | Recall format |
| Safe-area guard for bottom caption and right buttons | Text not covered by UI |
| Reel log: skip rate, average watch time, sends per reach when exposed | Observational creative testing |
