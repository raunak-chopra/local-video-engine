## Finding

The engine has a credible deterministic production foundation and useful motion
planning. Its reviewed examples show clear typography and stable composition,
but do not demonstrate a broad creative range or dependable execution under a
separate art director. Documentation previously encouraged creative exploration
without a concrete director-to-engine acceptance loop. This update adds that
loop, a focused Grill me mode and worked challenges. Their real-project success
remains to be tested.

The most consequential integration issue is capability drift: the Brand Manager
already directs a richer reel pipeline than this checkout implements. An
assistant that follows those instructions literally could promise nonexistent
commands, font support, overlays and audio mixing. Faithfulness requires surfacing
that gap, preserving the idea and planning the missing implementation.

`out/brand-manager-creative-preview/scenes.jpg` (seven settled scenes). These
are existing ignored artifacts, not newly rendered evidence of today's changes.

large white left-aligned headlines, coral accents, small brand header and a
bottom progress line. Hierarchy is consistent. “Explore. Choose. Refine.” and
“Keep what you approve.” are described as text rather than visibly demonstrated

suggests a future creative opportunity; it does not prove no such action occurs
between samples. The sheet cannot establish movement, pacing or reading time.

These are generic/historical films; the neighboring brand's current no-kicker
rule does not retroactively invalidate them. Neither artifact proves readiness
for its current cooking style. Actual kitchen footage, approved fonts, action
continuity and sound must carry that evaluation.
