# Envato Elements shopping list (cooking reels, 30-60 s, vertical)

Tick items off, drop files in the matching `assets/` folder, and log each in
`LICENSES.md`. Download only what the list asks for; a small, consistent kit
beats a big messy one.

Rules of thumb
- Video: 4K or 1080p, MP4/MOV, **vertical (9:16) preferred**; horizontal 4K is
  OK because I can crop to 9:16.
- Do NOT download After Effects, Premiere, Final Cut or DaVinci templates
  (.aep, .prproj, .mogrt, .drp). Remotion cannot use them.
- Motion graphics must be a rendered clip. Best: MOV (ProRes 4444) or WebM
  with alpha channel. Second best: black or green background clips that I can
  blend or key.

## 1. Fonts (do these first, they set the look)  -> `fonts/`
Hindi (Devanagari) and Latin/Hinglish must both be covered.

| # | Need | Search terms | Must have |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bold headline font, **Devanagari** | "Devanagari font", "Hindi font", "Marathi font" | Real Devanagari glyphs incl. conjuncts (क्ष, त्र, ज्ञ), Bold or Black weight |
| 2 | Bold headline font, **Latin** to pair with #1 | "bold display font", "poster font" | Used for English and Hinglish |
| 3 | Clean body/caption font, Devanagari | "Hindi sans serif" | Regular + Medium + Bold |
| 4 | Clean body/caption font, Latin | "modern sans" | Regular + Medium + Bold |
| 5 | Optional accent: handwritten or brush, Latin (Devanagari if you can find one) | "brush script", "handwritten" | For "tip" or "secret" labels |

Formats: TTF/OTF/WOFF2. Note the exact name in `LICENSES.md`.
Free alternatives I can use if Envato has gaps: Noto Sans Devanagari, Hind, Poppins.

## 2. Music  -> `music/`
| # | Need | Search terms | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-3 | Upbeat food/lifestyle tracks, 90-130 BPM | "cooking", "food", "upbeat acoustic", "happy lifestyle" | Clear beat, no vocals |
| 4-5 | Warm/cozy or desi-flavour tracks | "Indian", "Bollywood instrumental", "tabla", "sitar lofi" | For regional dishes |
| 6-7 | Trendy/lo-fi or punchy hip-hop beats | "lofi hip hop", "trap beat", "phonk light" | For fast "viral" style reels |
| 8 | Calm/ASMR bed | "ambient kitchen", "soft piano" | For slow, aesthetic cuts |

Prefer tracks that include **stems or short edits** (15 s / 30 s / 60 s).
No vocals, so the on-screen text stays readable. WAV or MP3.

## 3. Sound effects  -> `sfx/`
Cooking sounds (ASMR layers over the footage):
- sizzle, frying pan, oil bubbling, chopping on board, knife slice, pouring
  liquid, water boil, stirring spoon, spice shake/sprinkle, crunch, kitchen
  ambience, plate clink, cheese pull, steam.

Edit and UI sounds:
- whoosh/swish (short, medium, long), pop, bubble pop, click, tick,
  impact/hit, bass drop, riser (2-4 s), glitch, camera shutter, ding/bell,
  "text appear" blips, reverse swoosh.

Get a pack or two (e.g. "whoosh pack", "food ASMR pack", "UI pop pack")
instead of single files. WAV preferred.

## 4. Stock video, food  -> `broll/`
Vertical or 4K, no people's faces, ideally hands-only or food-only.

| Category | Search terms | Quantity |
|---|---|---|
| Ingredients, top-down or macro | "spices macro", "vegetables flat lay", "herbs", "chopping" | 15-20 clips |
| Cooking actions | "pan frying", "stirring pot", "pouring sauce", "kneading dough", "tadka", "steam rising" | 20 clips |
| Indian dishes, plated hero shots | "biryani", "dal", "paneer", "curry", "chai", "roti", "thali", "street food" | 15-20 clips |
| Kitchen ambience, backgrounds | "kitchen background", "rustic table", "marble countertop" | 5-8 clips |
| Slow-motion food | "slow motion pour", "slow motion spice", "flour dust" | 5-8 clips |
| Transitions made of food | "flour explosion", "smoke", "steam transition", "splash" | 5-8 clips (alpha or on black) |

Your own footage always takes priority; this is filler and hook material.

## 5. Motion graphics and overlays  -> `motion-graphics/`, `overlays-and-luts/`
All as rendered clips (alpha if possible).
- Lower thirds / title bars, simple and clean (3-5)
- Animated arrows, circles, underlines, highlight strokes, brush swipes
- Burst/starburst, sparkles, sticker-style badges ("NEW", "TIP", "HOT")
- Ingredient list backgrounds: card, paper, torn-paper, chalkboard (looped)
- Number/step badges (1-9) if available as a pack
- Light leaks, film grain, dust (3-5 each), as MP4 on black for screen blend
- Subscribe / follow / like / save animated buttons (vertical-friendly)
- Progress bar or timer overlays

## 6. Graphics and images  -> `images-and-logos/`
- Hand-drawn doodle packs: chilli, onion, tomato, garlic, spoons, steam lines,
  sparkles, arrows (SVG or PNG with transparency)
- Paper, wood, marble, chalkboard, and fabric texture backgrounds (JPG)
- Gradient or grain backgrounds in your brand colors
- Plate/bowl/pan mockup assets only if you want illustrated steps
- Your logo/watermark: SVG or transparent PNG

## 7. Colour (optional)  -> `overlays-and-luts/`
- 3-5 `.cube` LUTs for warm food grading (e.g. "food LUT", "warm cinematic").
  Pick one as your signature look.

## 8. Meme templates for 3-10 s reels  -> `memes/`
Short, punchy reels for recall: a relatable kitchen moment, a meme reaction,
and a text punchline in Hindi, Hinglish or English.

Envato Elements rarely carries real meme-format clips, so source these from
wherever you hold the rights; log each in `LICENSES.md`.

| Type | Examples to collect | Format | Quantity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reaction clips (short, 1-4 s) | shocked, disappointed, "wah", facepalm, evil laugh, confused | MP4/MOV, ideally 720p+ | 15-25 |
| Image templates | two-panel "expectation vs reality", "me vs mom", drake-style, split reaction | PNG/JPG, clean (no existing caption) | 10-15 |
| Audio stings | dialogue or tune clips (1-5 s), "bruh", laughing, dramatic hit, tabla/dhol hit | MP3/WAV, trimmed | 15-25 |
| Cooking-meme situations | burnt food, over-salted, "mummy ke haath ka khana", "guests arrived", "one more minute" | any of the above | as needed |
| Stickers/emoji | "wow", fire, chef-kiss, crying-laugh | PNG with transparency | 10-20 |

Tip: keep clips short and name them by mood (`shocked-01.mp4`,
`wah-sting-01.mp3`) so I can pick by intent.

## 9. References  -> `references/`
2-3 reels from cooking channels whose pacing and text style you like (files or
links in `links.txt`).

5. A few motion-graphics overlays  6. Everything else as needed.

With 1-4 I can already build the text system, clip scene and beat sync.
